## 1. Quick Reference Keybindings

| Key / Input | Action | In-Game Mechanics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **W, A, S, D** | Movement | Moves character relative to current camera viewpoint |
| **Mouse Move** | Camera Orbit | Third-person free look. Left-click anywhere in game to capture pointer lock |
| **Left Click** | Light Attack Combo | Chained 3-hit combo (Hit 1: 18 dmg, Hit 2: 24 dmg, Hit 3: 34 dmg + knockback) |
| **Right Click** or **E** | Heavy Attack | Overhead slam dealing 56+ damage. Staggers elite enemies and breaks shields |
| **Space** or **Shift** | Dodge Roll | Fast evasive roll with 0.42s invulnerability frames (i-frames). Costs 15 Energy |
| **Q** or **F** | Elemental Skill | Triggers current stance skill (Flame Wave / Glacial Spikes / Thunder Dash). Costs 25 Energy |
| **R** | Elemental Ultimate | Triggers apocalyptic screen-wide ultimate. Costs 50 Energy |
| **1** | Fire Stance | Equips Infernal Blade (Molten aura, burn damage, high DPS) |
| **2** | Ice Stance | Equips Frost Reaper (Crystalline aura, slowing & freezing crowd control) |
| **3** | Storm Stance | Equips Thunder Warden (Electric aura, chain lightning & high mobility) |
| **Mouse Wheel** | Cycle Elements | Instantly cycles through elements clockwise or counter-clockwise |
| **Tab** or **Middle Click** | Lock-On Target | Locks third-person camera orientation onto nearest enemy or boss |
| **Escape** | Pause Menu | Opens pause menu, halting combat and freeing mouse cursor |

## 2. Advanced Combat Techniques

### 2.1 Combo Chaining & Buffering
The game uses a **Combo Buffer Window**. When executing Light Attack 1 or 2, clicking again between 45% and 100% of the animation cycle automatically buffers and unleashes the next combo strike without delay.
- **Hit 1 (Right-to-Left Slash)**: Fastest attack, excellent for checking aggressive enemies.
- **Hit 2 (Upward Diagonal Slash)**: Carries forward momentum.
- **Hit 3 (Overhead Cleave)**: Features forward lunge and guaranteed knockback on infantry.

### 2.2 Exploiting Invulnerability Frames (i-Frames)
The Dodge Roll (`Space`) grants full damage immunity for the first 0.42 seconds of the animation.
- When The Fallen King or an Elite Knight raises their weapon for a heavy ground slam (indicated by glowing red ground telegraph circles), do not run away—**dodge right as the strike lands** to roll through the shockwave ring without taking damage, leaving you in prime position to counter-attack!

### 2.3 Elemental Strategy & Matchups
- **Fighting Swarms (Wave 1)**: Use **Fire (1)** for continuous burn DoT or **Storm (3)** to chain lightning across multiple enemies simultaneously.
- **Fighting Armored Elites (Wave 2)**: Switch to **Ice (2)** to reduce Elite Knight movement speed by 50%, or use the Ice Skill (`Q`) to freeze them solid for 2.4 seconds, opening them to uninterrupted Heavy Attacks (`Right Click`).
- **Fighting The Fallen King (Wave 3)**:
  * In Phase 1, wait for the ground slam telegraph, roll through the shockwave, and execute heavy attacks.
  * In Phase 2, switch to **Storm (3)** to use Thunder Dash (`Q`) to close the gap when he teleports away.
  * In Phase 3, trigger your Ultimate (`R`) during his meteor cast animation to deal massive burst damage and stagger him before the storm concludes.

### 2.4 Energy Management
- Energy regenerates passively at 8 units per second.
- Successful melee hits immediately grant bonus energy chunks, rewarding aggressive, precise offensive play.
- Always retain at least 15 Energy for emergency dodge rolls.
