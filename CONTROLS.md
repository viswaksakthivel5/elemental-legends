## 1. Quick Reference Keybindings

| Key / Input | Action | In-Game Mechanics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **W, A, S, D** | Movement | Moves warrior relative to current camera viewpoint (Walk / Run) |
| **Mouse Move** | Camera Orbit | Third-person free look. Left-click anywhere in game to capture pointer lock |
| **E** | **Interact** | **Read story clues/journals, inspect mechanisms, light braziers, freeze canal, power conduit** |
| **Left Click** | Light Attack Combo | Chained 3-hit combo (Hit 1: 18 dmg, Hit 2: 24 dmg, Hit 3: 34 dmg + knockback) |
| **Right Click** | Heavy Attack | Overhead slam dealing 56+ damage. Staggers elite enemies and triggers puzzles |
| **Space** or **Shift** | Dodge Roll | Fast evasive roll with 0.42s invulnerability frames (i-frames). Costs 15 Energy |
| **Q** or **F** | Elemental Skill | Triggers current stance skill (Flame Wave / Glacial Spikes / Thunder Dash). Costs 25 Energy |
| **R** | Elemental Ultimate | Triggers apocalyptic screen-wide ultimate. Costs 50 Energy |
| **1** | Fire Stance | Equips Infernal Blade (Molten aura, burn damage, lights village braziers) |
| **2** | Ice Stance | Equips Frost Reaper (Crystalline aura, slows & freezes, freezes canal into ice bridge) |
| **3** | Storm Stance | Equips Thunder Warden (Electric aura, chain lightning, charges fortress conduit) |
| **Mouse Wheel** | Cycle Elements | Instantly cycles through elements clockwise or counter-clockwise |
| **Tab** or **Middle Click** | Lock-On Target | Locks third-person camera orientation onto nearest enemy or boss |
| **Escape** | Pause Menu | Opens pause menu, halting gameplay and freeing mouse cursor |

## 2. Adventure Progression & Environmental Puzzles

### 2.1 Zone 1: The Forgotten Village (Puzzle 1: Fire Braziers)
1. Read the **Village Elder's Torn Journal** [E] on the pedestal near the entrance.
2. Select **Fire Stance [1]**.
3. Locate the 3 ancient stone braziers situated near the village cottages.
4. Strike each brazier with your sword, cast *Flame Wave* [Q], or press [E] while standing next to it.
5. Once all 3 braziers blaze with fire, the cursed thorn brambles dissolve and the forest gate opens.

### 2.2 Zone 2: The Haunted Forest (Puzzle 2: Canal Freeze)
1. Proceed through the forest, clearing patrolling Cursed Wanderers and Void Wraiths.
2. Read the **Scholar's Frost Parchment** [E] near the canal bank.
3. Select **Ice Stance [2]**.
4. Stand at the canal bank and cast *Glacial Spikes* [Q] or press [E].
5. The boiling corrupted canal instantly freezes into a solid crystalline ice bridge, granting safe passage to the Ancient Shrine.

### 2.3 Zone 3: The Ancient Shrine (Puzzle 3: Storm Conduit)
1. Defeat the shrine guardians among the gothic stone ruins.
2. Inspect the **Warden's Runic Inscription** [E] near the broken portcullis.
3. Select **Storm Stance [3]**.
4. Stand beside the copper conduit generator and cast *Thunder Dash* [Q], strike the terminal, or press [E].
5. High-voltage electricity surges through the coils, raising the iron portcullis into the ceiling.

### 2.4 Zone 4: The Ruined Fortress (The Hollow Guardian Boss)
1. Enter the grand fortress sanctum to trigger the final confrontation against **The Hollow Guardian**.
2. **Phase 1**: Dodge through telegraphed leaping slams and counter-attack with heavy slashes.
3. **Phase 2**: Use Storm Dash to close the distance when he teleports, and Ice to slow his flurry attacks.
4. **Phase 3 (Enraged Calamity)**: Unleash your Ultimate [R] when he channels dark meteor strikes to break his posture and finish the battle.
5. After defeating the boss, the curse dissolves into light and the victory summary is displayed!
