## 1. Quick Reference Keybindings

| Key / Input | Action | In-Game Mechanics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **W, A, S, D** | Movement | Moves warrior relative to current camera viewpoint (Walk / Run) |
| **Mouse Move** | Camera Orbit | Third-person free look. Left-click anywhere in game to capture pointer lock |
| **V** or **C** | **Echo Vision** | **Signature Mystic Sense**: Activates ethereal cyan sight revealing hidden runes, secret relic chests, spectral guiding footprints, and exposed enemy/boss weak points (2x critical strikes) |
| **E** | **Interact** | Read story clues/journals, inspect mechanisms, light braziers, freeze canal, power conduit, open relic chests, and enter exit portals |
| **Left Click** | Light Attack Combo | Chained 3-hit combo (Hit 1: 18 dmg, Hit 2: 24 dmg, Hit 3: 34 dmg + knockback) |
| **Right Click** | Heavy Attack | Overhead slam dealing 56+ damage. Staggers elite enemies, damages boss, and triggers winch mechanisms |
| **Space** or **Shift** | Dodge Roll | Fast evasive roll with 0.42s invulnerability frames (i-frames). Costs 15 Energy |
| **Q** or **F** | Elemental Skill | Triggers current stance skill (Flame Wave / Glacial Spikes / Thunder Dash). Costs 25 Energy |
| **R** | Elemental Ultimate | Triggers apocalyptic screen-wide ultimate. Costs 50 Energy |
| **1** | Fire Stance | Equips Infernal Blade (Molten aura, burn damage, ignites village braziers and left winch) |
| **2** | Ice Stance | Equips Frost Reaper (Crystalline aura, slows & freezes, solidifies canals and cools right winch) |
| **3** | Storm Stance | Equips Thunder Warden (Electric aura, chain lightning, charges electrical dynamo conduits) |
| **Mouse Wheel** | Cycle Elements | Instantly cycles through elements clockwise or counter-clockwise |
| **Tab** or **Middle Click** | Lock-On Target | Locks third-person camera orientation onto nearest enemy or boss |
| **Escape** | Pause Menu | Opens pause menu, halting gameplay and freeing mouse cursor |

## 2. Five-Chapter Campaign Walkthrough

### 2.1 Chapter I: The Forgotten Village
- **Objective**: Ignite 3 Ancient Braziers using Fire [1], clear patrolling Cursed Villagers, and activate the ancient Exit Portal.
- **Echo Vision Secrets**: Reveals the glowing Rune Pillar hidden beside the well and spectral tracks guiding directly to the exit gateway.
- **Story Clue**: *The Elder's Journal* detailing the descent of the unnatural fog and corrupted brambles.
- **Permanent Reward**: **Talisman of Vitality** (+25 Max HP).

### 2.2 Chapter II: The Whispering Forest
- **Objective**: Solidify the Corrupted Acid Canal with Ice [2], eliminate the Forest Stalkers and Void Wraiths, and breach the forest gate.
- **Echo Vision Secrets**: Unveils a hidden clearing holding the *Frostfire Relic Chest* nestled behind dead oaks.
- **Story Clue**: *Scholar's Frost Parchment* uncovering the alchemical properties of the corrupted canal bile.
- **Permanent Reward**: **Glacial Focus** (-1.0s Elemental Skill Cooldown).

### 2.3 Chapter III: The Sunken Shrine
- **Objective**: Overcharge the subterranean Lightning Dynamo Conduit with Storm [3], defeat the armored Corrupted Sentinels, and open the deep shrine gateway.
- **Echo Vision Secrets**: Highlights submerged electrical conduits and stone glyphs carved into the water-logged walls.
- **Story Clue**: *Warden's Inscription* recording the origin of the corruption within the ancient elemental dynamos.
- **Permanent Reward**: **Storm Conduit Mastery** (+15% Critical Hit Chance).

### 2.4 Chapter IV: The Hollow Fortress
- **Objective**: Solve the Dual Elemental Winch Lock (Fire on the left winch, Ice on the right winch), defeat the elite Fortress Warmaster champion, and unlock the portcullis.
- **Echo Vision Secrets**: Reveals the secret armory cache containing the *Aegis of the Fallen King* relic.
- **Story Clue**: *Commander's Last Decree* exposing the grim truth: the guardians did not fall to external monsters, but were corrupted from within.
- **Permanent Reward**: **Warrior's Resolve** (+25 Max Energy).

### 2.5 Chapter V: The Heart of the Curse (Grand Finale)
- **Objective**: Confront and destroy **The Hollow Guardian** across 3 dynamic combat phases atop the floating Abyssal Dais.
- **Phase 1**: Evade telegraphed ground shockwave slams and land precision counter-strikes.
- **Phase 2**: Boss channels dark void barrages; activate **Echo Vision [V]** to expose his glowing Soul Core weak point for double critical damage.
- **Phase 3 (Cataclysm)**: Evade falling meteors, survive the void storm, and execute your Ultimate [R] to deliver the final strike and purge the realm's curse forever!

## 3. Save & Progression System
- **Automatic Campaign Saving**: Every completed level, unlocked chapter, discovered clue, acquired relic, and permanent stat modifier is preserved automatically in browser `localStorage`.
- **Level Select**: Replay any previously completed chapter anytime from the main menu without losing campaign progress or reset state.
- **New Game Option**: Reset campaign progress cleanly via the title menu confirmation modal to start a fresh journey.
